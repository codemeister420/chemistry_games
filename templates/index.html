Chemistry Games

Learn chemistry by playing — structure, reactivity, and controlled chaos.

  • Functional Group Quiz
    Identify functional groups on randomly generated molecules. Train pattern recognition and chemical intuition.
  • Isomer Quiz
    Decide whether two molecules are identical, isomers, or unrelated. Covers structural and stereoisomerism.
  • Carbohydrate Snake
    Story: You’re a carbon snake trapped inside a lipid vesicle. Touch the bilayer and you die — unless you eat F (fluorine), which makes you membrane-permeable for a short time.
    Mechanics: C= restricts rotation, O/N cause branching, X slows movement.
  • pH Dodger
    Dodge acids and bases to keep your pH between 0 and 14. Grab buffers to resist swings (strongest near pKa), survive CO2 dissolution, and stabilize chaos in solution.
  • Organic Reaction Lab (Bio Edition)
    Choose biochemical processes and instantly see the RDKit-drawn product. Includes phosphorylation, methylation, acetylation, glycosylation, lipidation, and Cu²⁺ complexation — with starters like phosphatidylserine, amino acids, peptides, sugars, glycerol, and sterols.
Built with Flask, RDKit, and questionable chemical life choices 🧪
